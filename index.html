poems & thoughts

I write what I can’t say out loud.

It’s raining somewhere inside. Touch the glass. Scroll slowly. The night gets darker as you go.

I · thought
What a ridiculous creature man is, spending half his life longing for what he does not possess,
and the other half grieving what he has already lost.
— Aditya Chauhan
II · grief
Days have started to grow shorter, it’ll be night soon,
but in the darkness of the night grief knocks at my door, night brings me closer to the things I spend my days trying to forget.
The longer the night, the deeper the grief, the deeper the grief, the colder the heart becomes.
— Aditya Chauhan
III · home
If I had to choose between my next breath and your name,
I would use my last breath to call you home.
— Aditya Chauhan
IV · one night, one thought
One night, I’ll think of you, and one thought I’ll write, I’ll write myself as a scar, and you as the moonlight.
I’ll write of a silver moon and winds that softly blow, I’ll write your hand in mine, beneath a sky aglow.
Perhaps in reality, we’ll never meet, So I’ll write our first encounter in a book of dreams.
In that book, everything will happen as I please, I’ll turn the day into night, and write you fast asleep.
One night, I’ll think of you, and one thought I’ll write, I’ll write myself as a scar, and you as the moonlight.
Perhaps you’ll never be mine in this lifetime, Perhaps you’ll never be mine in this lifetime, So I’ll write our meeting in a book, Where fate cannot draw the final line.
— Aditya Chauhan
